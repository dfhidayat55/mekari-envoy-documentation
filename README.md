# How to use Mekari Envoy

Mekari Envoy is Mekari's AI workspace. You chat with an AI assistant, get files made for you, connect the apps you already use, and set up helpers that do recurring work on a schedule.

The built-in assistant is called **Airene**. You can also chat with **agents**: helpers your team has set up for a specific job, with their own instructions, knowledge and connected apps.

This guide set has one guide per feature. Start with **[Getting started](guides/getting-started.md) Getting started**, then open the guide for the feature you want to use.

## Find your way around

You sign in with **Sign in with Google**, using your work Google account. Only people an admin has added can get in; anyone else sees **Access Not Granted** (see [Getting started](guides/getting-started.md)). After you sign in, the left rail holds the main areas. The bell and your account menu sit in the top-right corner. What you see depends on your role, so some items may be missing for you.

| On screen | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Chats** | Talk to Airene or to an agent. A new chat starts with **Airene Assistant**. Your past chats are listed here. | [Chatting with Airene](guides/chatting-with-airene.md), [Agents](guides/agents.md) |
| **Tasks** | Ask an agent to do something on a schedule, for example a weekly summary. | [Tasks](guides/tasks.md) |
| **Library** | Files Envoy made for you: slide decks, documents, spreadsheets and pages. | [Library](guides/library.md) |
| **Agents** | Find, chat with and create agents. | [Agents](guides/agents.md) |
| **Connectors** | Connect your apps (Mekari apps, Google, Microsoft and more) so Envoy can work with them. Admins also set up the Slack and Google Chat bots and Qontak Communication here. | [Connectors (connect your apps)](guides/connectors.md), [Morning Brief & Meeting assistant](guides/morning-brief-and-meeting-assistant.md), [Envoy in Slack & Google Chat](guides/envoy-in-slack-and-google-chat.md) |
| **Settings** | Skills, knowledge base, your AI data and notification choices, and, for admins, people, company AI controls (including the monthly AI budget) and technical pages. | [Knowledge base](guides/knowledge-base.md)–[Your AI data & memory](guides/your-ai-data-and-memory.md), [Managing people](for-admins/managing-people.md), [Company AI controls & Connector access](for-admins/company-ai-controls-and-connector-access.md) |
| Bell (top right) | Your notifications. **View all** opens the Inbox. | [Inbox & notifications](guides/inbox-and-notifications.md) |
| Account menu (top right) | **Switch company** (only if you belong to more than one), **Theme** (System, Light or Dark), **Install Envoy app** (only when your browser offers it; it installs Envoy on your computer, not on your phone) and **Sign out**. It also shows your Company ID. | [Getting started](guides/getting-started.md) |

## Not covered yet

These parts of Envoy exist but are not ready or not meant for everyday staff yet, so this guide set leaves them out. They will be added when that changes.

| Feature | Why it is not in this guide set |
|---|---|
| Voice mode (talking to Airene) | Switched off for now. The voice buttons do not appear. |
| Roles & permissions (custom roles) | Switched off for now. Access uses the three standard roles: Admin, Member and Viewer (see [Getting started](guides/getting-started.md)). |
| Platform-only pages: Platform settings, Platform owners, User activity, Usage & Cost, Usage History, Model Roles | Only the small team that runs Envoy for all of Mekari can open these. |
| Putting an agent inside Talenta or Officeless ("Embed in a product" and "Company knowledge" on the agent page) | Hidden from the agent page since 19 October 2026. Agents that were already set up keep working in Officeless (see [Envoy inside Mekari products](guides/envoy-inside-mekari-products.md)). The chat panel inside Talenta is not available yet. |
| Experts gallery | Hidden from the menus because it overlaps with Agents. Use the **Agents** page instead ([Agents](guides/agents.md)). |
| Web search | Controlled by a platform-wide setting that is off unless the Envoy team turns it on, so it may not be available to you. |
| Staff inbox for live website chats | Retired. The **Inbox** page now holds your notifications ([Inbox & notifications](guides/inbox-and-notifications.md)). |
| Design-system reference pages | An internal reference for the people who build Envoy. Not linked from the app. |
| Envoy mobile app | Being tried out inside Mekari and not released. **Install Envoy app** in the account menu installs Envoy on your computer, not on your phone. |
| Ticket assistant on the Qontak CRM **Tickets** page | A pilot that the Envoy and CRM teams switch on company by company. If it is switched on for your company, see [Envoy inside Mekari products](guides/envoy-inside-mekari-products.md). |
| Slack connector (agents working in Slack as you) | Listed on **Connectors**, but it can't be connected until the Envoy team finishes registering the Slack app. This is different from the Slack bot ([Envoy in Slack & Google Chat](guides/envoy-in-slack-and-google-chat.md)). |
| Microsoft SharePoint connector | Listed on **Connectors**, but it can't be connected yet. |
| Browser pop-up notifications | Not switched on yet. Your notifications still appear under the bell and in the Inbox ([Inbox & notifications](guides/inbox-and-notifications.md)). |

## Words you will see

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Airene** | Envoy's built-in AI assistant. |
| **Agent** | A helper set up for a specific job, with its own instructions, knowledge and connected apps. |
| **Connector** | A link between Envoy and another app, such as Google Calendar or Mekari Talenta. Each person connects their own account. |
| **Skill** | A reusable "how to do X" package that Airene or an agent can use. |
| **Knowledge base** | Files and web pages you add so answers can come from your own material. |
| **Library** | Where the files Envoy made for you are kept. |
| **Task** | Work an agent does for you on a schedule. |
| **Inbox** | Your list of notifications, such as finished tasks and meeting briefs. |
