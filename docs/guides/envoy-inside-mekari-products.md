# Envoy inside Mekari products

{% hint style="success" %}
**General Guide** — for everyone, no technical knowledge needed.
{% endhint %}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who this is for** | Everyone whose company has Envoy switched on inside Qontak CRM or Officeless |
| **Where to find it** | The side tab on the edge of the page in Qontak CRM, or the **Airene** chat panel inside Officeless. Both appear only where your company has them switched on. |
| **Last checked** | 24 October 2026 |

## What it is

Envoy can sit inside some Mekari products, so you can ask your assistant without leaving the page you are working on. It is switched on company by company, so you may not see it yet. There are two versions, and they look a little different.

In Qontak CRM, Envoy opens as a side panel from a tab on the edge of the screen. It is a pilot that the Envoy and CRM teams switch on for each company, starting with the **Tickets** page. It works with your Qontak CRM sign-in, so it can only see and change what you can see and change in CRM. You chat with the agents your admin offers there, and the assistant can see what the page shares with it.

In Officeless, Envoy opens as a chat panel called Airene unless your company renamed it. You are already signed in through the product. This panel keeps your chat history, accepts files, and shows where its answers came from. The same panel is being prepared for Talenta but is not available there yet.

## Before you start

* Your company has to have Envoy switched on in the product. If you don't see the tab or the panel, ask your admin.
* For Qontak CRM: an Envoy account that an admin has added to your company. If the panel asks you to connect, you sign in to Envoy once in a small window. It doesn't have to be the same email as your Qontak CRM login.
* Allow pop-ups for the product's site, so the sign-in window can open.
* For Officeless: just be signed in to the product.

## How to use it

### Open the side panel in Qontak CRM

1. Click the tab on the edge of the page. It shows the assistant's name.
2. The panel opens beside the page, and the page moves over to make room.
3. To move the tab, drag it up or down. It stays where you leave it.
4. Click **Expand** to make the panel wider, and **Collapse** to make it narrow again.
5. Click **Close panel**, or press Esc, to close it.

{% hint style="info" %}
The panel remembers whether you left it expanded.
{% endhint %}

### Connect your account if asked (Qontak CRM)

1. Type your question, or click a suggestion. If a card above the message box says **Sign in to Envoy to chat with your assistant here**, you need to connect once.
2. Click **Connect**. A sign-in window opens.
3. Sign in to Envoy if asked (today that is **Sign in with Google**, with your work Google account). The window lists the agents and connectors Envoy will use for you. Click **Allow**.
4. When you see **You're connected — you can close this window.**, go back to the panel. The message you typed is sent for you.

{% hint style="info" %}
If the window doesn't open, click **Didn't open? Continue here** to connect in the same tab.
{% endhint %}

### Chat in the Qontak CRM panel

1. On an empty chat the panel greets you by name ("Hi <your name>, I'm here.") and may show **Suggested actions**. Click one to send it.
2. Type in **How can I help you today?** and press Enter. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
3. If **Looking at:** shows above the message box, the assistant can see that item from your page, so you can ask about it directly.
4. If an answer ends with reply buttons, click one to answer. Answers about numbers can include a chart.
5. If the assistant wants to change something in an app, it shows a card with **Confirm** and **Cancel**. Nothing runs until you click **Confirm**.
6. If the assistant makes a file (a slide deck, document, spreadsheet or notes), a card appears under the answer. Click its download button to save it.
7. Click **New chat** (the plus) to start fresh.

### Switch agent or disconnect (Qontak CRM)

1. Click the agent's name at the top of the panel to see the agents on offer. The first one is marked **Default**.
2. Pick another agent. If your chat already has messages, click **Start new chat** to confirm. Switching always starts a new chat.
3. To unlink your Mekari account, click **Account** (the three dots), check the **Connected as** line, and click **Disconnect**.

### Use the chat panel in Officeless

1. Open the assistant from the product.
2. Pick a prompt under **Try asking**, or type in **How can I help you today?** and press Enter.
3. To add files, click **Attach files** (the paperclip), drag files onto the message box, or paste an image. Wait until each file has finished uploading before you send.
4. While an answer is being written, click **Stop generating** to stop it.
5. Under a finished answer, use **Copy answer**, 👍 (**Good answer**), 👎 (**Bad answer**) or **Regenerate**. Answers can include charts and tables.
6. Under your own message, click **Edit and send again** to put the text back in the box. If your message got no answer, click **Send again**.

{% hint style="info" %}
The paperclip only appears when the agent accepts files.
{% endhint %}

### Check sources, approvals and choices (Officeless)

1. When an answer has sources, numbers like [1] in the text can be clicked. Click one, or click **Sources**, to see the list.
2. Click **View** to open a source inside the panel, or **Download** to save it. Some documents open in their own tab through **Open document**.
3. When the assistant wants to run an action, a card says what it will do. Click **Show details** to see the values, then click **Confirm** or **Cancel**.
4. When it asks you to choose, click an option. If you can pick several, tick them and click **Send answer**.

### Go back to an earlier chat (Officeless)

1. Click **Chat history** at the top left of the panel.
2. Find your chat under **Recent**, **This week**, **Older** or **Archive**, or type in **Search chats** to search titles and text.
3. Click a chat to open it, or click **New chat** to start a new one.

## Your options

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Expand** / **Collapse** | Qontak CRM only. Switches the side panel between 440 and 720 pixels wide. |
| Agent picker (the agent's name in the header) | Qontak CRM only. Lists the agents your admin offers here. Picking another one starts a new chat. |
| **Suggested actions** / **Try asking** | Ready-made questions on an empty chat. Your admin can change them. |
| Status under the title | Officeless shows **Online**, **Working…** while an answer is written, or **Waiting for you** when an approval or a choice needs your answer. |
| **Regenerate** | Writes the answer again. On the latest answer it runs at once. On an earlier answer it first asks **Regenerate this answer?**, because it removes the messages after that answer. |

## Good to know

* In Qontak CRM a message can be up to 4,000 characters. A counter appears from 3,500 characters.
* The Qontak CRM panel doesn't take attachments. In Officeless you can attach up to 5 files per message, each up to 20 MB: PNG, JPEG, GIF or WEBP images, PDF, Word (.docx), TXT, CSV and Markdown files.
* Connecting in Qontak CRM is a one-time step. Later visits need no clicks, and you can disconnect at any time from the **Account** menu. After you connect, your panel chats are private to you and also appear in your chat list in Envoy, named after the panel.
* A message you type before connecting is held ("Will send after you connect") and goes out once you are connected.
* If you send many messages very quickly, the Qontak CRM panel shows **Too many messages.** with a countdown. Wait for it to finish.
* In Officeless a chat can be open in only one browser tab. The newest tab takes over; older tabs show **This chat is open in another tab.** and become read-only until the other tab closes.
* **Archive** in Chat history simply holds chats you haven't touched for about a month. It isn't something you do to a chat. Empty chats aren't listed.
* In Officeless an unsent draft stays in the box if you reload the page.
* On a narrow window (under 900 pixels wide) the Qontak CRM panel covers the page instead of sitting beside it.
* AI can make mistakes. Check important information before you act on it.

## Ideas to try

* With a ticket open in Qontak CRM and **Looking at:** showing it, ask: "What should I do next here?"
* Click **Make a slide deck** in Suggested actions and download the deck it makes.
* In Officeless, ask: "Compare this month with last month as a chart."
* Attach a PDF in Officeless and ask: "List the key dates in this file."

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Envoy isn't available for your company right now." | Envoy hasn't been set up for this product in your company. Ask your admin. |
| The sign-in window says "Envoy isn't set up for your company yet. We'll ask your admins." | Add an optional **Note for your admin** and click **Request access**. The panel then says it asked your admin; click **Check again** later. |
| "Sign-in isn't available on this page." | The page couldn't confirm who you are. Reload it. If it keeps happening, tell your admin. |
| The sign-in window says "Ask your admin to add you to <company>." | Your Envoy account isn't in that company yet. Ask your admin to add you on **Users** ([Managing people](../for-admins/managing-people.md)), or click **Use a different account**. |
| "Your connection ended. Connect again to continue." | Click **Connect** and sign in again. This happens when your access changed or your admin turned the link off. |
| "Your browser blocked the sign-in window" | Allow pop-ups for this site, then click **Try again**. |
| The sign-in window says "This account is linked to another Envoy account" | Click **Use a different account** and sign in with the account that is already linked, or disconnect it there first. |
| "Your session has ended" (Officeless) | Sign in to the product again, then reopen the panel. |
| "Your message was not sent. It is back in the box; try again." | Check your connection and send it again. |
| "This chat is open in another tab." | Use the other tab, or close it. This tab takes the chat back when the other one closes. |
